Neon Harbor is a 2D top-down open-world crime game that runs offline in a desktop browser. You are new in a sunny port city, broke, and one bad decision from rich: steal cars, run five story jobs for Rosa and Marlo, dodge the cops, and end up running the city.

What is in the world:

- A full city to explore on foot or by car — avenues, streets, sidewalks, a park with a pond, and a harbor with piers and a beach.
- Traffic and pedestrians living their own lives on the road grid.
- A wanted system: stealing cars, running people over, and shooting at cops raises your stars, and police cars spawn and chase you until the heat cools off.
- Five story missions with a minimap marker and an on-screen arrow: steal a first car, run a crate from the pier, lose a two-star tail, pick up a cash drop on foot, and deliver the ledger to the penthouse at three stars.
- Combat: aim with the mouse, shoot, reload, and ram. Health, ammo, cash pickups, and medkits.
- HUD with health, cash, ammo, wanted stars, and a live minimap; start screen, pause, a wasted screen, and progress saved in localStorage.
- Synthesized sound only — engine hum, police sirens, gunshots, and mission jingles, all built with the Web Audio API. No audio files, no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.

Controls: WASD to move and drive, mouse to aim, click to shoot, E to steal or exit a car, R to reload, P to pause, M to mute, N for a new game.
